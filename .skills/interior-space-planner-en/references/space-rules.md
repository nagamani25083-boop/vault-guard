# Space Planning Rules
Classify facts as user-confirmed, drawing-derived, photo-inferred, or site-verification. Only the first two support hard dimensional decisions.
Check door/window/cabinet swings, clearances, and entry/activity/storage/service routes. Record accessibility, child, older-adult, and pet needs. Structural, gas, fire, electrical, plumbing, waterproofing, and code changes go to professional review.
Use one primary style and at most one support direction. Prompts include dimensional assumptions, camera position, layout anchors, materials, and light.
