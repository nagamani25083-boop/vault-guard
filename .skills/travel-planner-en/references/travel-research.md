# Destination Research Guidelines

## Information layers

Separate:

| Type | Treatment |
|---|---|
| User input | Label as user-provided |
| Model suggestion | Label as a suggestion, not a fact |
| External fact | Record source and lookup date |
| Estimate | Show range, currency, and assumptions |
| Unresolved item | List clearly; do not state as certain |

## Source priority

1. Government, immigration/customs, embassy, health, and official travel-advisory sources.
2. Airport, rail, public transit, attraction, and operator websites.
3. Reliable weather services and local tourism agencies.
4. Reliable media or platforms as secondary sources.
5. Reviews, forums, and social media only as experience context.

## Research checklist

- Entry: verify nationality, origin, transit points, destination, documents, visa, stay limits, and health materials.
- Health: verify official health notices, medication, and vaccination rules; do not replace medical advice.
- Safety: verify current advisories, emergency numbers, and location-specific risks; never guarantee safety.
- Weather: show forecast date, destination timezone, and the difference between climate averages and forecasts.
- Transit: verify schedules, fares, baggage rules, transfers, reservations, and service status.
- Attractions: verify opening hours, prices, taxes, reservations, and official ticket channels.
- Practical information: currency, payments, communications, power, language, and accessibility.

## Research output

```markdown
| Item | Finding | Source | Lookup date | Timezone/scope | Unresolved items |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
```

When sources conflict or cannot be accessed, explain the conflict or mark the item unverified. Prices must include currency, unit, taxes/fees, and lookup date.
