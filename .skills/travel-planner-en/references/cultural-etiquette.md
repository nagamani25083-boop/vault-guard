# Cultural Etiquette and Venue-Rule Research Template

> This is a research template, not a fixed database of facts about countries, regions, or religions.

## Verification scope
Specify the country, city, specific venue, activity, and lookup date. Prefer official institutions, venue notices, and reliable local sources.

## Research dimensions

- Greetings, titles, queues, personal space, and communication;
- dress in public, business, beach, and religious settings;
- dining, dietary restrictions, service charges, and tipping;
- religious activities, access, prayer times, footwear, photography, and restricted areas;
- photography of people, museums, military/government sites, and drone rules;
- alcohol, medication, prescriptions, public conduct, transport, and other legal restrictions;
- LGBTQ+ laws and safety information;
- accessibility facilities and venue assistance.

## Output template

```markdown
# [Destination] Etiquette and Venue Rules
Lookup date: [YYYY-MM-DD]
Scope: [country/city/specific venue]

## Verified rules
- [Rule] — Source: [institution/page] — Applies to: [scope]

## Non-mandatory customs
- [Custom] — Source: [source] — Individual/regional variation: [note]

## Unresolved items
- [Conflicting, outdated, or unverified information]
```

Do not make absolute claims about entire continents or religions. Cultural guidance does not replace legal, medical, or on-site safety instructions. Specific venue rules override generic travel advice.
