# Styling Rules
Satisfy weather, dress code, activity, and comfort first. Explain looks through color relationships, silhouette balance, material contrast, and formality.
Give one visual focus to each outfit. Substitutions preserve function. Evaluate capsules by compatible combinations, not garment count. Packing favors rewear, layering, easy care, and activity coverage.
A shopping gap requires repeated need, insufficient owned coverage, budget fit, and at least three compatible looks.
