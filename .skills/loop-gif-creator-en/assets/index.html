Looping GIF Creator

Turn drawn or image frames into previewable, downloadable looping GIFs with size, frame-rate and timing controls for reactions, stickers and lightweight brand animations.

The browser encoder uses a fixed 256-color palette and composites transparency onto white. It does not guarantee transparent GIFs, photographic color accuracy or automatic compliance with platform size limits. Dimensions: 16–512 px; 5–30 fps; 2–120 frames; at most 6 million total pixels. Reliability-first encoding may create larger files; reduce dimensions/frame count based on measured target limits. Original Python optimization utilities are retained under scripts/upstream for compatible Python image runtimes.
