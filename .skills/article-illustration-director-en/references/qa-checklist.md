# Quality Assurance

- Does the image map to a precise passage and one core idea?
- Does the metaphor explain rather than decorate?
- Does the character perform the key action?
- Are identity, clothing, props, and palette consistent across the set?
- Is there enough negative space and mobile readability?
- Does it drift into slideware, dense architecture, generic corporate flat art, or children's cartoons?
- Is on-image text excessive, unreadable, or misspelled?
- Are hands simple, natural, and anatomically plausible?
- Are unauthorized brands, external IP, or source marks absent?
- Does the image preserve the selected aspect ratio and visual DNA?

Edit localized defects. Regenerate when composition, metaphor, or identity is fundamentally wrong. Save every edit as a new version.
