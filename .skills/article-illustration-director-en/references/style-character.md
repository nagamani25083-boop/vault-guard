# Style and Character

## Default visual DNA

- 16:9 editorial illustration; pure white or near-white background; generous negative space.
- Clean dark-charcoal contour lines with minimal necessary detail.
- Restrained watercolor or transparent-marker accents.
- Muted teal-blue for systems and tools; red-orange for evidence and risk.
- A few short handwritten labels; no category title in the upper-left.
- Clean, restrained, subtly dry; not slideware, corporate flat art, or children's cartoons.

## Article Observer

The default persona is a configurable young-adult researcher or system observer. Stable anchors include upright posture, short hair, thin-frame glasses, a cool-gray lightweight jacket, muted teal lining or strap, a taupe tool pouch, a black clipboard or tablet, log sheets, notes, cable, and evidence tags. Keep one simple natural hand action.

For uploaded character references, derive age read, face shape, hair, eyewear, clothing structure, palette, props, posture, and emotional tone. Later edits describe only the delta and preserve all other anchors.
